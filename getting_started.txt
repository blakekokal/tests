GETTING STARTED


1. ENTERING A ROUND

You can log your rounds two ways: Shot by Shot or Hole by Hole.

Shot by Shot
- Tap "New Round" and select your course
- For each shot, enter the lie (tee, fairway, rough, sand, green, etc.) and the distance to the hole
- After each shot, the app calculates your expected strokes from that position based on your handicap benchmark
- Continue logging shots until you hole out, then move to the next hole
- This gives you the most detailed Strokes Gained breakdown — you'll see exactly where you're gaining or losing strokes

Hole by Hole
- Tap "New Round" and select your course
- For each hole, just enter your total score and basic shot info (fairways hit, greens in regulation, putts)
- This is faster if you don't want to track every shot during your round
- You'll still get Strokes Gained data at the hole level, but without the shot-by-shot detail

Tip: Shot by shot entry gives you the best insights. If you're short on time during a round, you can always enter hole by hole and switch to shot by shot when you're practicing or playing a casual round.


2. CREATING AN ACCOUNT & LINKING COACH/STUDENT CODES

Creating Your Account
- Download the app and tap "Sign Up"
- Enter your name, email, and set a password
- Set your handicap — this is used for all your Strokes Gained benchmarks

Linking as a Student
- Go to your Profile or Settings
- Tap "Link to Coach"
- Enter the coach code your coach gave you
- Once linked, your coach can view your rounds, stats, and progress

Linking as a Coach
- Go to your Profile or Settings
- Find your unique coach code and share it with your students
- Once a student enters your code, they'll appear in your dashboard
- You can view all of their rounds, drill into shot-level data, and track their improvement over time

Tip: Coaches can manage multiple students from one account. Students can be linked to one coach at a time.


3. READING YOUR STATS & CHANGING HANDICAP COMPARISONS

Understanding Strokes Gained
- Strokes Gained shows how many strokes you gain or lose compared to a benchmark handicap
- Positive numbers = you're performing better than the benchmark in that area
- Negative numbers = you're losing strokes compared to the benchmark
- Categories include: Off the Tee, Approach, Short Game, and Putting

Your Dashboard
- After logging rounds, your dashboard shows your overall Strokes Gained trends
- Tap into any category to see a detailed breakdown
- You can view stats per round, over a date range, or as a rolling average

Changing Handicap Comparisons
- By default, your stats compare against your own handicap benchmark
- To change the comparison, go to your Stats page and tap the handicap selector
- You can compare yourself against any handicap level (scratch, 5, 10, 15, 20, etc.)
- This is useful for seeing what it would take to get to the next level — for example, if you're a 10 handicap, compare against a 5 to see where you need to improve most

Tip: Regularly check your stats after a few rounds to spot trends. Changing the handicap comparison is a great way to set goals and see exactly what areas of your game need work to drop to the next level.
