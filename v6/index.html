Help & Info
Getting Started
1. Make an account to save your rounds and stats.

2. Set your handicap comparison. In Settings, under your student code, you can set which handicap level your Strokes Gained data is compared against.

3. Share your student code with your coach (optional). This allows your coach to view all of your past rounds and stats, and allows them to send you drills and combines.

4. My Bag. Enter your clubs along with the carry and total distance for each club to keep track of your yardages. You can also do a wedge gapping session to find out how far your 1/2 and 3/4 wedge swings go.
Round Entry
Entering a New Round
1. Tap Enter New Round.
2. Your SG comparison auto-populates from your profile. You may change it here as well.
3. Search for a course and select a teebox to auto-populate pars and yardages. If the course isn't available yet, you can enter a custom course manually and save it for later.
4. Choose a round type — Tournament or Casual — for stat tracking.
5. Enter the number of holes: 9, 18, or a custom set. You can also enter 18 holes and end the round early if you're unable to finish all 18.

3 Types of Round Entry

Simple — No detailed stats. Just pars, yardages, and scores for fast and easy entry.

Hole by Hole — All stats available. For each hole you enter each shot by selecting the end lie and how far you had left into the green. For example: Shot 1 — end lie fairway, 150 yards; Shot 2 — end lie green, 25 feet; and so on.

Shot by Shot (Recommended) — All stats available. The app walks you through each shot, prompting you for the lie and distance to the hole after every shot. This is the preferred entry method and gives you the most detailed Strokes Gained breakdown.
Past Rounds
View a log of all your past rounds. These are also visible to your coach if you've shared your student code. You can upload previously saved rounds here as well. Use the search bar to find rounds by course name, filter by round type or date range, and sort by score or other criteria.
Stats Dashboard
A detailed overview of all your stats broken into the main Strokes Gained categories: Off the Tee, Approach, Short Game, and Putting. You can change the handicap comparison and filter by round type to zero in on specific areas of your game.
Performance Analysis
An overview of the main Strokes Gained categories that highlights your trends and shows where you need the most improvement. This is where we recommend you look first to decide what to work on in practice. Coaches also use this page to determine which drills to assign and which part of your game to focus on. Performance analysis also covers your past combine results so you can track skill-specific progress over time.
My Bag
Club Distances — Enter the clubs in your bag along with the carry and total distance for each. You can also find your averages for each club by entering a set number of shots and letting the app calculate them for you.

Wedge Gapping — Select your wedges and the gapping system you use (1/2 swings, percentages, or body positions), then hit shots to see how far your wedges carry for all your partial swings.

Speed Sessions — Log a speed session to track your club head speed and ball speed over time.
Practice & Learning
This is where all the drills, combines, tests, and games live, along with tools to help you learn and improve as a golfer.

Combines — Test your skills from set distances. For example, a putting combine takes you through a set number of putts from different distances and breaks, logging your score and SG value from each session. Combines are available for wedge carries, chipping, bunker play, driver, and more.

Practice Log — Log your practice sessions so your coach can see what you're working on at each session.

Learn Tab
• SG Calculator — See the Strokes Gained value of specific shots. For example: How much does a 300-yard drive gain you vs. a 10 handicap? How far do you need to hit it on par 5s to gain strokes?
• Putting Break Calculator — Uses Aimpoint values and vector putting to estimate break and aiming point based on green speed and slope.
• Optimizer — Enter your clubs to see recommended distances, spin rates, carry, height, loft, and attack angle to optimize your performance.
Coaching
For Coaches
The Coaching tab is your hub for managing all of your students. Once a student shares their student code with you, they appear in your dashboard. From there you can:
• View each student's rounds, stats, and performance trends
• Send drills, combines, and practice plans directly to a student
• Track their improvement over time and adjust training focus
• Use the Performance Analysis page to identify where each student needs the most work and assign targeted drills accordingly

For Students
Once you've linked to your coach by sharing your student code, you'll receive drills and combines assigned by your coach directly in the app. Complete them and your results are automatically shared back so your coach can track your progress and adjust your training plan.

Guide to Golf
How to hit every shot
Driver
  • Feet wide
  • Ball by front foot
  • Tall tee
  • Shoulders up
Hybrid / 3 Wood
  • Feet slightly wider than shoulder width
  • Ball slightly forward in stance
  • Shoulders level
  • 55-65% of weight on back foot
Irons
  • Feet shoulder width
  • Ball in middle of stance
  • Shoulders level
  • 50/50 weight on each foot
Chipping (Low)
  • Use PW or SW
  • 70-80% of weight on front foot
  • Feet closer together
  • Ball in back of stance
  • Putting motion, no wrists
Chipping (High)
  • Use SW
  • 70-80% of weight on front foot
  • Feet closer together
  • Ball in front of stance
  • Putting motion, no wrists
Bunker / Sand
  • Use SW
  • Feet shoulder width
  • Ball in front of stance
  • 70-80% weight on front foot
  • Big swing, hitting sand 1 inch behind ball
Putting
  • Feet around shoulder width
  • Ball in middle/front of stance
  • No wrists, rock back and shoulders back and forth

3 P5 520: LR 260, F 95, GB 12, G 8, H
F fairway · G green · LR / RR left / right rough · FB fairway bunker · GB greenside bunker · MG missed green · R recovery · W water · OB out of bounds · H holed
