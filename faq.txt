ENTERING ROUNDS

Q: What's the difference between shot by shot and hole by hole entry?
A: Shot by shot tracks every individual shot with lie and distance, giving you the most detailed Strokes Gained breakdown. Hole by hole is faster — you just enter your score, fairways hit, greens in regulation, and putts for each hole.

STATS & HANDICAP COMPARISONS

Q: How do I change the handicap I'm comparing against?
A: Go to your Stats page and tap the handicap selector. You can compare your performance against any handicap level — scratch, 5, 10, 15, 20, etc.

Q: Why would I compare against a different handicap?
A: It helps you set goals. For example, if you're a 15 handicap and want to get to 10, comparing against a 10 shows you exactly which parts of your game need the most improvement.

Q: How many rounds do I need before my stats are useful?
A: You'll start seeing data after your first round, but trends become more reliable after 3-5 rounds. The more rounds you log, the clearer the picture.

Q: What do positive and negative Strokes Gained numbers mean?
A: Positive means you're performing better than the benchmark in that category. Negative means you're losing strokes compared to the benchmark.
